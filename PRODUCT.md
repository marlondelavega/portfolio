# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two equal primary audiences visiting one personal portfolio:
- Hiring managers and employers deciding whether to interview or hire for a full-stack developer role.
- Government clients and agencies deciding whether to commission web-based information systems or event and campaign design work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Marlon de la Vega, a Full-Stack Developer and Graphic Designer with 3 years of experience. It presents who he is, his development and design skills, and his work. Success means a visitor understands the combined dev and design capability quickly and gets in touch.

## Positioning

One person who both builds web-based information systems for local government (citation management, investment planning tools) and designs the logos, layouts, and materials for government events and campaigns.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on desktop and mobile, skim the left column (bio, skill cards, contact), and go to the main work panel. The site is evaluated quickly, often between other candidates or proposals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page SvelteKit site (static adapter); the main panel is currently a placeholder labeled "My works" and is not yet populated.
- Left column: header with logo and buttons (My works on mobile, Info, Contact me), bio card, Development / Design skill toggle with a single skill list.
- Info and Contact open dialogs. Contact uses the email address below.
- Terminology: "Development" and "Design" are the two skill groups.
- Undecided: content and layout of the "My works" panel.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Marlon de la Vega (keep exactly).
- Experience claim: "3 years of experience" (keep exactly).
- Profile photo at src/lib/assets/photos/me.jpg is a real photo and should be kept.
- Contact email marlondelaveg4@gmail.com is the real public contact address.

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner states real project work exists. No project files, screenshots, or paths are in the repository yet; future work must not invent project examples, clients, or outcomes.
- Skill proficiency levels are intentionally left blank; do not add levels or claims about proficiency without the owner's input.

## Product Principles

- Show the dev and design combination immediately; neither discipline should read as an afterthought.
- Credibility over decoration: every claim traces to a real project or a confirmed fact.
- Fast to scan: a visitor should find the bio, skills, and contact in seconds.
- Keep the owner's real identity (name, photo, experience claim) intact across any redesign.
